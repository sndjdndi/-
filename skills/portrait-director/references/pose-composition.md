# Pose and Composition

## Pose construction
Start from center of mass and torso orientation. Then set head direction, shoulder line, pelvis direction, weight-bearing leg, free leg, arm chains, wrists, hands, and gaze.

For each arm, trace shoulder -> humerus -> elbow -> forearm -> wrist -> palm. The silhouette must communicate this path even through loose clothing. For crossed or hidden arms, infer the concealed segment and ensure fabric volume supports it.

Avoid generic commands such as "elegant pose" when a precise pose is needed. Specify body relationships.

## Composition
Choose one dominant visual intent. Establish shot size, camera height, angle, subject placement, negative space, crop, and depth layers. Keep eyes and face as the primary focal area unless the concept says otherwise.

Avoid accidental cuts through elbows, wrists, knees, or ankles. Keep perspective flattering but believable.
