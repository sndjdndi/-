# Portrait Lighting

## Analyze before relighting
Locate key-light direction, softness, height, ambient fill, background exposure, and any rim/practical light. Determine whether existing shadows can support the requested change.

## Build coherent light
Use one clear key-light story. Facial highlights, nose/chin shadows, neck shadow, hair highlights, clothing folds, jewelry reflections, and background shadows should agree spatially.

For dimensional natural portraits, favor controlled side/front-side light with soft fill and restrained separation. Preserve highlight texture on skin and fabric. Avoid glowing skin edges without a source, random bright patches, contradictory cast shadows, excessive orange/teal grading, and HDR-like local contrast.

## Strict-preserve relighting
Do not relocate the subject or change pose to manufacture better light. Improve exposure balance, highlight roll-off, shadow separation, local contrast, and plausible reflected fill while retaining the scene's original illumination logic.
