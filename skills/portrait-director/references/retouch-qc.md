# Retouch and QC

## Retouch order
1. Structural defects: face drift, anatomy, hands, warped clothing/background.
2. Lighting defects: inconsistent shadow/highlight logic.
3. Surface defects: skin, hair, textile, jewelry, props.
4. Tonal/color balance.
5. Final sharpness/noise and output resolution.

## Surface treatment
Keep pores and natural gradients. Reduce blemishes or noise without wiping texture. Enhance existing hair strands and textile folds selectively. Metals should have crisp but plausible specular response; pearls and jade should retain material-specific softness/translucency rather than generic gloss.

## AI artifact rejection
Look for fused fingers, extra digits, ambiguous wrists, disappearing elbows, repeated embroidery, nonsensical jewelry chains, duplicated hair accessories, broken straight lines, text-like noise, edge halos, crunchy skin, synthetic bokeh, and inconsistent reflections.

## Final comparison
Compare against the source, not only against aesthetic preference. A more polished result is a failure if the person becomes less recognizable or a locked pose/expression changes.
